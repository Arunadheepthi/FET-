WELCOME TO THE NILIGIRIS

Ooty, officially called Udhagamandalam, is a famous hill station in the Nilgiri Hills of Tamil Nadu, known for its cool climate, lush tea plantations, misty mountains, and scenic landscapes. Popular tourist attractions include the Nilgiri Mountain Railway (a UNESCO World Heritage Site), Ooty Lake, Government Botanical Garden, Doddabetta Peak, Rose Garden, Pykara Lake and Waterfalls, and the expansive Avalanche and Emerald valleys. Visitors enjoy boating, trekking, wildlife spotting in nearby sanctuaries, shopping for homemade chocolates and eucalyptus products, and tasting locally grown Nilgiri tea. The best time to visit is October to June , making Ooty one of South India's most popular destinations for nature lovers, families, and honeymooners.
